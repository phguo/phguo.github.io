Profile

Penghui Guo ("郭鹏辉" in Chinese) earned his PhD in management science and engineering from College of Economics and Management, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA), China. He earned his master's degree in management science and engineering (industrial engineering) from NUAA, and his bachelor's degree in industrial engineering from Henan Polytechnic University, China.

Penghui Guo's main research focus is on humanitarian logistics, specifically the modeling methods and algorithms under uncertainty for humanitarian relief. He has published several papers in peer-reviewed journals including Naval Research Logistics, IISE Transactions, European Journal of Operational Research, and Systems Engineering - Theory & Practice ("系统工程理论与实践" in Chinese).

Penghui Guo developed a Python library for Benders decomposition, namely BendersLib. His current research interest is in developing software packages for decomposition algorithms in mathematical programming. He aims to make these methods more accessible to researchers and practitioners, thereby contributing to the optimization software ecosystem.
